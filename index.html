Our Story

Bottled at dawn, when the absolutes are honest.

Aurora began in a two-room atelier with a copper still, a shelf of traveller's samples, and one stubborn belief: a fragrance should be composed the way a letter is written — deliberately, and for one reader.

Every batch is macerated for six weeks, rested, and bottled by hand. No shortcuts, no seasonal churn — just eight signatures, perfected.
